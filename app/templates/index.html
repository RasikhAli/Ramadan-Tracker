Recommended Duas

Iftar Dua - 1
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي لَكَ صُمْتُ وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ وَعَلَىٰ رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ
O Allah, I fasted for You and believed in You and I break my fast with Your sustenance
Allahumma inni laka sumtu wa bika aamantu wa 'ala rizqika aftartu
Sunan Abi Dawud 2358
Iftar Dua - 2
ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ، وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ، وَثَبَتَ الأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
The thirst is gone, the veins are moist, and the reward is established, insha'Allah
Dhahaba adh-dhama'u, wabtallatil-'urooqu, wa thabata al-ajru in shaa Allah
Sunan Abi Dawud 2357
Sehri Intention
وَبِصَوْمِ غَدٍ نَّوَيْتُ مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ
I intend to fast tomorrow in the month of Ramadan
Wa bisawmi ghadin nawaiytu min shahri Ramadan
Commonly cited in South Asian tradition
Note: The intention (niyyah) is an action of the heart. Scholars agree it should be made before fasting, but verbal recitation is optional.
